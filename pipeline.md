## Stages
- `target` — identified, not yet contacted
- `outreach sent` — cold email sent, awaiting reply
- `replied` — they responded, next step TBC
- `screening` — recruiter / first call scheduled
- `interviewing` — active rounds
- `offer` — offer received
- `closed` — rejected / withdrawn / accepted
